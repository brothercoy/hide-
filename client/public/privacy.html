PRIVACY POLICY

hide  ·  hide-ascii.com  ·  last updated 22 September 2026

hide is a free browser game. It has no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics. This page describes everything it does with information, which is very little.

WHAT IS KEPT ON YOUR DEVICE

Your progress and settings are saved in your own browser, using its local storage. They stay on your device. They are never uploaded, and nobody else can read them.

  • Your chosen colour theme and your volume settings.
  • Your campaign progress: which levels you have completed, how many lives you have, and which secrets you have found.
  • Your result for the current daily level.
  • A short-lived token that lets you rejoin a multiplayer game if your connection drops.
  • The name you last typed, remembered for the current browser session only.

Clearing your browser's site data erases all of it, including your campaign progress. There is no copy anywhere else, so there is nothing to restore it from.

Each place you play keeps its own separate save. Progress on this website, on itch.io, and inside Discord are three different save files and do not sync.

WHAT THE SERVER SEES

The server exists to run multiplayer games. It holds a game only while it is being played.

  • The name you choose, so other players in your lobby can see who is who. It exists for the life of that game and is discarded when the game ends.
  • What you do during a round, such as which character you tapped, so the game can be scored. This is not kept after the game.
  • Ordinary connection records. Like any web server, it is reached over the internet and the hosting provider logs requests, which include IP addresses. These are operational logs, not a profile of you.

Nothing is written to a database or a file. When a game ends, it is gone.

PLAYING INSIDE DISCORD

hide can be launched as a Discord Activity. When you do that, Discord asks your permission to share your account details with the game. If you agree, the game receives your Discord display name and account ID.

  • Your display name is used for one thing: showing who is in the lobby, so nobody has to type a name. It is treated exactly like a typed name above.
  • The access token Discord issues is passed straight through and is never stored. It exists only long enough to ask Discord for your name.
  • The game does not read your messages, your servers, your friends, or anything else. It requests a single permission, identify, and nothing more.

You can withdraw this at any time in Discord, under User Settings, Authorized Apps.

What happens on Discord's side is covered by Discord's own privacy policy, not this one.

WHAT IS NOT DONE

  • No cookies are set.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party scripts.
  • No advertising.
  • Nothing is sold, shared, or handed to anyone.
  • No email address or password is ever asked for, because there are no accounts.

CHILDREN

hide is suitable for all ages and collects nothing that could identify a child. Discord requires its own users to be at least 13, or older where local law says so.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Since the game holds nothing about you, there is no back catalogue of data for a change to apply to.
